Delivery Zones and Times

Say Saya — Eat Sushi! We bring the experience to your door.

Great news! We deliver to all districts of Astana. Your sushi arrives fresh, carefully packed in our eco-friendly O2-permeable containers.

We guarantee 100% quality from our kitchen directly to your door.

Our Reliable Delivery Process

We know that when you order sushi, you expect it to be fresh, beautiful, and on time. That is why we have built an in-house fleet of dedicated couriers who understand the importance of careful handling. We do not use third-party delivery apps because we want to maintain strict quality control over every order.

Once you submit your order, our kitchen receives it instantly. The chefs prepare your rolls precisely when the courier is ready to depart. This synchronization ensures that hot items like our baked rolls stay warm in thermal bags, while cold items remain perfectly chilled. On average, our delivery takes between 45 to 60 minutes depending on your district, but we always strive to beat the clock without sacrificing safety.
