Our Reliable Delivery Process

We know that when you order authentic hot dishes, you expect them to be steaming, delicious, and on time. That is why we have built an in-house fleet of dedicated couriers who understand the importance of careful handling. We do not use third-party delivery apps because we want to maintain strict quality control over every order.

Once you submit your order, our kitchen receives it instantly. The chefs prepare your lagman and shashlyk precisely when the courier is ready to depart. This synchronization ensures that hot items stay warm in thermal bags, while fresh salads remain perfectly chilled. On average, our delivery takes between 45 to 60 minutes depending on your district, but we always strive to beat the clock without sacrificing quality.
