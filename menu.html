Terminology

Lagman
A traditional Central Asian dish of pulled noodles, meat, and vegetables.
Plov / Pilaf
A fragrant rice dish cooked in a seasoned broth with meat and vegetables.
Shashlik
Skewered and grilled cubes of meat, similar to shish kebab.
Kazan
A traditional cast-iron cooking pot used throughout Central Asia, akin to a Dutch oven or cauldron.
