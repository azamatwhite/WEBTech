Our History & Concept

How Dastarkhan Started in Astana

The story of Dastarkhan restaurant began in 2005 with a simple yet strict concept: serving authentic Eastern and Kazakh national cuisine using traditional recipes and fresh local meat. We started as a cozy oriental cafe, and thanks to authentic flavors, we quickly earned the trust of Astana residents.

We purchase fresh halal meat delivered daily from local farms and use traditional spice blends like Zira & Star Anise. Every dish, from handmade lagman to fragrant plov, is cooked in classic cast-iron kazans according to original traditions.

Our goal is to bring people together around a generous dastarkhan, where traditional food, warm hospitality and a comfortable atmosphere make every visit memorable.

Interesting Kitchen Fact

The dough for our handmade lagman noodles is pulled by hand and cooked in boiling water at approximately 100°C, helping create its distinctive texture.
