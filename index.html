Experience Authentic Flavors

Welcome to Saya Sushi, your ultimate destination for fresh, high-quality rolls and Japanese cuisine in Astana. Whether you are craving our signature baked rolls or a quick, delicious Sushidoner, our chefs prepare every dish with passion and top-tier ingredients.

Our Story and Dedication to Quality

At Saya Sushi, our journey began with a simple idea: to bring the authentic taste of premium Japanese cuisine to the heart of Astana without compromising on speed or quality. We believe that great sushi starts with great ingredients. That is why we source our salmon fresh, use only the highest grade of short-grain sushi rice, and prepare every single roll to order. Whether you are craving the classic Philadelphia roll, our signature baked options, or our innovative Sushidoner fusion, we promise an unforgettable flavor experience every time you dine with us.

Our commitment to excellence has earned us a loyal following across the city. Do not just take our word for it—our customers say it best:
