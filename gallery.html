Restaurant Interior and Dish Details

Atmosphere and Interior Design

Modern Japanese Comfort Concept

The interior of Saya Sushi is designed in a minimalist style featuring natural wood elements and soft warm lighting. We created a space that is equally comfortable for a quick business lunch or a cozy evening dinner.

Special attention is given to layout zoning: from an open sushi bar to comfortable sofas for large groups.

Atmosphere Note

Background Lo-Fi music plays in the dining area, while lighting adapts according to the time of day: brighter during daylight and dim in the evening.
