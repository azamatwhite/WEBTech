Our History & Concept

How Dastarkhan Started in Astana

The story of Dastarkhan restaurant began in 2005 with a simple yet strict concept: serving authentic Eastern and Kazakh national cuisine using traditional recipes and local fresh meat. We started as a cozy oriental cafe, but thanks to authentic flavors, we quickly earned the trust of Astana residents.

We purchase only fresh halal meat delivered daily from local farms and use traditional spice blends like Zira & Star Anise. Every dish, from handmade lagman to fragrant plov, is cooked in classic cast-iron kazans according to original traditions.

Interesting Kitchen Fact

The dough for our legendary handmade lagman noodles is pulled by hand and cooked at a temperature of exactly 100°C, ensuring its unique authentic chewy texture.

Taste Philosophy & Quote

“Hospitality is the core of our tradition. If the dough for lagman was pulled this morning, those exact fresh noodles will be served in your bowl tonight. Real flavor tolerates no compromises.”

— Head Chef Serik, personal interview during a visit on February 10, 2026.

Key Stages of Development

  1. Opening of the first oriental cafe on Mukhtar Auezov Street.
  2. Expanding the main dining hall and opening a second flagship venue on Dostyk Street.
  3. Introducing an updated author's menu featuring traditional Turkish and Uygur specialties.

Eastern Cuisine Glossary

Dastarkhan
A traditional Central Asian tablecloth or dining space where guests gather to share a feast.
Shero
Specifically seasoned noodle broth infused with fresh herbs, garlic, and traditional eastern spices.
