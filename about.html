Our History & Concept

How Saya Sushi Started in Astana

The story of Saya Sushi began in 2022 with a simple yet strict concept: preparing traditional Japanese cuisine without using frozen fish. We started as a small takeaway kitchen, but thanks to high-quality ingredients, we quickly earned the trust of local residents.

We purchase only fresh chilled salmon delivered via direct flights and use specialized Akita Komachi rice. Every dish is prepared strictly after receiving an order to preserve the natural flavor and texture.

Interesting Kitchen Fact

The rice for our sushi rolls is served at a temperature of exactly 37°C (human body temperature), which allows its taste profile to fully unfold.

Taste Philosophy & Quote

“We never use deep freezing. If the salmon arrived this morning, that exact fish will be in your roll tonight. Flavor tolerates no compromises.”

— Head Chef Askar, personal interview during a visit on January 14, 2026.

Key Stages of Development

  1. Opening of the first takeaway kitchen on Dostyk Street.
  2. Expanding the dining hall and launching full delivery across Astana.
  3. Introducing an author's menu featuring baked and tempura rolls.

Japanese Cuisine Glossary

Omakase
A traditional ordering style where guests entirely entrust dish selection to the head chef.
Shari
Specifically prepared sushi rice seasoned with rice vinegar, salt, and sugar.
